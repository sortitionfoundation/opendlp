The house family is Lucide, taken from the Icons sheet of the OpenDLP - UI Figma file, at stroke-width 2 with round caps and joins. Need a new icon? Take it from that sheet and add a macro here; do not draw your own. The three small-grid glyphs at the bottom are drawn to fill the stepper bubble and the checkbox square, which is deliberate. Brand marks, the product logo, diagram illustrations and the GOV.UK pagination arrows are deliberately not here.
