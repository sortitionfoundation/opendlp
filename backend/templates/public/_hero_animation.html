{# ABOUTME: The home page hero animation: an abstract browser window in which everyone invited appears as dots, ABOUTME: targets are set, one click runs the lottery and the chosen dots take their seats in an assembly semicircle. #} {# Inline SVG, animated by the .hero-draw rules in static/backoffice/src/main.css: one 17 s loop that every element keys to, so nothing drifts. Each dot carries its own appearance delay (--d); a chosen dot also carries the offset to its seat (--dx, --dy). The seat outlines are drawn first so the dots cover them. The dots are 64 people in three groups (plum, red, orange, half / a third / a fifth) and the panel of 20 keeps those proportions (10 / 6 / 4), which is the point of the drawing. Under prefers-reduced-motion the CSS shows the result at rest. Generated layout: the numbers come from a one-off script, but the file is plain markup and is edited by hand from here. #}
